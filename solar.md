## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if I need an electrician immediately?

If your lights are flickering, you smell burning near outlets or your switchboard, you've lost power to part of your home, or a circuit breaker keeps tripping -- that's urgent. Electrical faults aren't just inconvenient, they can be dangerous for your family. Our team responds quickly to emergencies. Call us on [0400 000 000](tel:0400000000) and we'll prioritise getting your power restored safely.

### Can you handle all types of electrical work?

We cover the full range of residential and commercial electrical services. That includes general wiring and powerpoints, ceiling fan installation and repair, lighting design and upgrades, switchboard replacements, air conditioning servicing and installation, emergency callouts, EV charger installations, and commercial electrical fit-outs. Every job is carried out by licensed electricians who know what they're doing.

### How long does it take to complete an electrical repair?

It depends on what's causing the problem. A faulty powerpoint might take 30 minutes while a full switchboard upgrade could take several hours. Once we've diagnosed the issue, we'll give you an honest timeframe and quote before starting any work. Our aim is fixing it right the first time so you're not dealing with the same issue next week.

### How can I prevent electrical problems in the future?

Regular inspections are your best protection. We recommend having your switchboard and wiring checked every few years, testing safety switches regularly, avoiding overloaded powerpoints, and addressing warning signs early like flickering lights or warm outlets. Catching small problems before they escalate saves you money and keeps your family safe. If you're unsure about anything, our team is always happy to provide straightforward advice.

We explain everything clearly, keep work areas clean, and back our workmanship completely. We're fully licensed, available 24/7 for emergencies, and committed to your satisfaction. When you call us, you're getting experienced electricians who take pride in keeping your home safe and powered.

### Do you handle urgent electrical problems outside regular hours?

Yes, we're available around the clock. Electrical faults don't wait for convenient times, especially during storms or extreme weather. Whether it's a power outage on a Saturday night or a sparking outlet on a public holiday, our emergency electricians are ready to respond. Call [0400 000 000](tel:0400000000) anytime, and we'll get someone to you as quickly as possible to restore your power safely.
